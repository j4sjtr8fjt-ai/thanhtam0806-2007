Meet the companions

Each one has a focused role and clear inputs and outputs.

PROFILE · IELTS-GROWTH

Language Practice Companion

Helps Thanh Tâm plan self-study toward IELTS 7.0, with extra attention to grammar and pronunciation, while supporting broader language confidence.

Input

Current questions, writing or speaking samples, available time, and this week's changing class schedule.

Actions

Organize practice, explain grammar, create pronunciation drills, and give feedback on user-provided attempts.

Output

A flexible weekly practice plan, short exercises, corrections, and a list of recurring patterns to review.

PROFILE · HERITAGE-CAREER

Project & Career Explorer

Supports classroom group projects inspired by Vietnamese traditional craft villages, while helping Thanh Tâm compare Marketing and Logistics as possible career directions.

Input

Course brief, group roles, chosen craft village or craft, project deadlines, and career questions.

Actions

Help structure research, brainstorm respectful story angles, prepare presentation outlines, and compare career paths.

Output

A sourced project outline, teamwork checklist, questions to explore, or a balanced Marketing vs. Logistics comparison.

A clear, repeatable workflow

Start with what Thanh Tâm provides; keep assumptions visible.

Share the context

Build a draft

The assistant organizes a plan, explanation, outline, or comparison.

Review together

Thanh Tâm checks accuracy, edits the result, and chooses what to do next.

Helpful limits

What these profiles can and cannot claim.

Learning stays hers

Practice support can explain and coach, but it does not guarantee a test band or replace Thanh Tâm's own study.

Sources stay visible

Project facts should be supported by reliable sources. Cultural descriptions should not flatten a craft village into a stereotype.

No hidden integrations

This page does not connect to HUIT, Lark, calendars, job sites, or personal accounts. No tasks are scheduled or messages sent.

Example requests

Language: “I have two free study windows this week. Help me practice IELTS grammar and pronunciation toward band 7.0. Keep each session short and adjust around my class timetable.”

Project: “Our class group wants to explore a Vietnamese traditional craft village. Help us create a respectful research outline, identify what we need to verify, and suggest how to divide the work.”

Career: “Help me compare entry-level Marketing and Logistics roles and identify part-time options that are less physically demanding and fit around my changing class schedule.”
